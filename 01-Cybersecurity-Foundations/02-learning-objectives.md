# Learning Objectives
By completing this course, I was able to :
- Recognize the fundamental knowledge and technical skills required for an entry-level cybersecurity analyst.
- Understand how cybersecurity incidents affect business operations.
- Explain the importance of ethics and professional responsibility in cybersecurity.
- Identify common cybersecurity tools used by security analysts.
