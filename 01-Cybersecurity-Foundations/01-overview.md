# 1. Course Overview: Foundation of Cybersecurity

## Introduction
**Foundation of cybersecurity** is the first course in the Google Cybersecurity Professsional certificate. It introduced me to the core principles of cybersecurity and helped me build a strong foundation for understanding how organizations protect their systems, networks and sensitive information. Troughout this course I developed the fundamental security mindset technical vocabulary and analytical thinking expected from an entry-level cybersecurity analyst.
